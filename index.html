Social & Local Impact

Significance to the Community

Democratizing computer vision turns everyday smartphones and laptops into assistive, educational, and problem-solving tools—without requiring expensive hardware or cloud subscriptions.

Accessibility & Everyday Inclusion

Instant visual recognition empowers community members with visual impairments, language barriers, or cognitive challenges to rapidly identify objects, signage, or materials in real time—fostering greater independence in shared public spaces.

Community Sustainability & Resource Care

Whether assisting residents with accurate waste sorting, detecting early signs of crop or plant stress in community gardens, or inspecting local infrastructure, fast visual triage reduces waste and protects shared neighborhood resources.

AI Literacy & Citizen Science

Interactive, transparent confidence bars demystify how machine learning works. Students, educators, and local volunteers can observe how lighting, angles, and training data shape model certainty—turning passive users into informed AI creators.

Privacy-First, Offline-Capable Edge AI

Powered by TensorFlow.js, every camera frame and uploaded photo is processed 100% locally inside the user's browser. Zero visual data is transmitted to external servers, safeguarding community privacy and minimizing bandwidth usage in low-connectivity areas.

Open & Replicable Architecture

Built for Zero-Cost Community Deployment

Hosted statically on GitHub Pages with zero backend servers to maintain, this web application can be freely shared via QR code at schools, barangay halls, libraries, and community workshops.
